# RetailPulse AI 2.0 — Phase 4 Business Intelligence

## Data source

All reported values come from the synthetic RetailPulse dataset.

- Observations: 70,000
- Stores: 10
- Products: 10
- Date coverage: 2024-01-01 through 2025-11-30
- Grain: one store-product-day observation

These results demonstrate an analytics workflow and must not be
presented as measured performance from a real retail business.

## Architecture

1. PostgreSQL fact and dimension tables
2. Five PostgreSQL BI reporting views
3. Python BI export pipeline
4. Five CSV exports and one JSON executive scorecard
5. Future dashboard consumers

## KPI definitions

### Net revenue

Sum of the dataset's net_revenue field.

### Estimated gross profit

Sum of gross_profit_estimate.

This is an estimated gross-profit measure, not audited net profit
or a complete accounting of operating costs.

### Estimated gross margin

100 * estimated gross profit / net revenue.

### Stockout observation rate

100 * stockout observations / total observations.

This measures the proportion of store-product-day records
with a stockout, not the percentage of customer visits
affected by stockouts.

### Unmet demand rate

100 * lost_sales_units / latent_demand_units.

Latent demand is available in this synthetic dataset because
it was generated by the simulation. It is not directly observable
in most real-world retail transaction data.

### Month-over-month revenue growth

100 * (current month revenue - previous month revenue)
    / previous month revenue.

The first month has no prior month, so growth is NULL.

### Category revenue share

100 * category net revenue / total net revenue.

Category percentages may not total exactly 100 due to rounding.

### Estimated gross lost-sales value

Sum of lost_sales_units * selling_price.

This is a hypothetical gross-sales opportunity estimate.
It is not realized revenue, guaranteed recoverable sales,
or an estimate of net profit.

## Promotion analysis caveat

Promotion and non-promotion comparisons are descriptive.
Differences do not establish that promotions caused changes
in demand, sales, or stockout rates.

## Reproducibility

Create or refresh PostgreSQL BI views:

    psql -X -v ON_ERROR_STOP=1 -f sql/schema/04_create_bi_views.sql

This command assumes the appropriate PostgreSQL connection
environment or authentication has already been configured.

Export the reporting datasets:

    python -m src.analytics.export_bi_data

Run quality checks:

    python -m ruff check config scripts src tests
    python -m pytest tests -q

## Outputs

The BI export pipeline writes:

- data/processed/bi/executive_kpis.csv
- data/processed/bi/monthly_performance.csv
- data/processed/bi/category_performance.csv
- data/processed/bi/store_performance.csv
- data/processed/bi/product_stockouts.csv
- data/processed/bi/executive_scorecard.json

The JSON scorecard serializes Decimal values as strings
to preserve their decimal representation.
